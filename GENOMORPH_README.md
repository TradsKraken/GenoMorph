# 🌿 GENOMORPH
> *Transform and Thrive. Keep the Wild Alive.*

---

## 🧠 What Is This Game?

GenoMorph is an **educational Action-RPG** where the player shapeshifts into real native animals to fight invasive species and restore a dying ecosystem.

The key idea is simple:
> **Real biology = your powers.** No magic. No fantasy. Everything the player can do comes from how real animals actually survive.

The player wears a high-tech suit called **The GenoMorph** that stores animal DNA and physically transforms the wearer into different native species. Each form plays completely differently — different movement, different skills, different strengths.

---

## 📖 The Story

### The Setup
A once-thriving natural region is collapsing — polluted, fragmented, and overrun by invasive foreign pests. A scientist named **Dr. Alistair Sterling** invents **The GenoMorph**, a suit that can store native animal DNA and safely transform the wearer into those animals.

Because the wilderness is too dangerous for him personally, Dr. Sterling recruits a brave **Nature Volunteer** (the player) to wear the suit. He stays in his lab and guides the player remotely through a built-in radio.

### What Goes Wrong
On the very first test run, the suit suffers a **catastrophic system failure**. A power surge wipes the entire DNA database. All the stored animal DNA scatters across the region — and the local invasive animals absorb it, mutating into massive, terrifying creatures called **Invasive Chimeras** (monsters made from mixed animal DNA).

The player is now:
- Stranded in a toxic swamp
- Stuck in the weakest form — a **Small Native Frog**
- With no other transformations available

### The Goal
Hunt down each Chimera, defeat it, and **extract the corrupted DNA** it absorbed. The suit purifies that DNA, unlocking a new animal form — which the player needs to reach the next area.

### How It Ends
After defeating all four Chimeras and recovering all the DNA, Dr. Sterling patches the suit back to full power. The player — now able to shift into any form freely — becomes the **ultimate Keystone Species**. The food web restores itself. The whole region comes back to life.

## 🔁 How a Level Works — The Ecosystem Hub

Every level is a **semi-open exploration hub**, not a straight path. The player clears areas progressively, finds multiple NPCs, and collects biological data to earn their skill — then fights the boss.

```
1. ARRIVE          →  Spawn in biome using current animal form

2. COMBAT EXPLORE  →  Navigate the map zone by zone
                      Clear Invasive Nests to restore the environment
                      and unlock the next zone

3. FIND NPCs       →  Locate 3–4 native species survivors hiding in the biome
                      Each NPC teaches a different ecological lesson
                      Each gives a combat challenge or minigame

4. EARN FRAGMENTS  →  Completing each NPC challenge awards a Genome Fragment
                      (a piece of biological data the suit needs)

5. SYNTHESIS       →  Collect all Genome Fragments
                      The suit synthesizes the level's Biological Action Skill
                      (e.g., Cutaneous Respiration, Thermal Soaring)

6. BOSS FIGHT      →  Enter a locked arena and fight the Invasive Chimera
                      The new skill is essential for surviving the boss

7. UNLOCK          →  Extract the Chimera's DNA → unlock the next animal form

8. ADVANCE         →  The next biome is physically blocked in a way only
                      the new form can pass
```

> **The physical barriers between biomes:**
> Frog can't fly over the highway → need the **Hornbill**.
> Hornbill can't dive into deep water → need the **Shark**.
> Shark can't smash mountain boulders → need the **Tamaraw**.

## 🗺️ The Four Levels

---

### Level 1 — The Polluted Wetlands
**Form:** 🐸 Small Native Frog
**Skill Synthesized:** Cutaneous Respiration
**Boss:** Snail-Toad Chimera

> 📄 **Full level design is in its own file:** [LEVEL_1_WETLANDS.md](./LEVEL_1_WETLANDS.md)

**The world:** A stagnant, murky toxic swamp. Everything is grey and dying. Color slowly returns as the player clears nests and completes challenges.

**The 4 NPCs (each gives one Genome Fragment):**
| NPC | Species | Lesson | Challenge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lolo Palaka | Elder Tree Frog | Frogs as bioindicators | Wave-clear: eat mosquito swarms before they infect the pond |
| Dalag | Mudfish Elder | Dissolved oxygen & pollution | Protect young mudfish from Tilapia school attacks |
| Tagak | Purple Heron | Trophic cascade / food chain | Escort Tagak safely across the Reed Beds |
| Bayawak | Water Monitor Lizard | Scavengers & nutrient cycling | Timed dash: tag decomposing spots before a bacterial bloom spreads |

**Skill synthesized — Cutaneous Respiration:**
Dash into water puddles to rapidly heal. Also emits a small AoE toxin pulse while in water (based on frog skin secretions).

**Boss — Snail-Toad Chimera:**
Slow but devastating. Fires toxic sludge volleys, slams with its heavy shell, and forces the player to use the water pools strategically to survive.

**What unlocks next:**
**Bird DNA** extracted → **Rufous Hornbill** form → fly over the concrete highway to the Rainforest.

---

### Level 2 — The Fragmented Rainforest
**Form:** 🦅 Rufous Hornbill *(Kalaw)*

**What the world looks like:**
A forest cut apart by illegal logging roads. The canopy is broken, and the land is scarred.

**What you do:**
- Experience dramatic verticality — the Hornbill is a heavy, powerful flyer
- Escort a smaller native bird safely across a dangerous logging camp while fighting off airborne pests
- Complete a minigame where you fly over the canopy, fight invasive insects, and drop fruit seeds precisely into fertile ground below *(teaches seed dispersal and forest succession)*

**Skill you earn:**
> **Thermal Soaring** — A vertical dash using warm air updrafts. Grants brief invincibility, letting you fly through sweeping ground attacks.
> *(Based on how large birds ride thermal columns to conserve energy and gain altitude.)*

**Boss: The Feral Hog-Rodent Chimera**
- Erratic charge attacks and rooting mechanics
- You use Thermal Soaring to dodge, then dive with your heavy beak to crack its armor

**What unlocks next:**
Defeating the boss gives you **Aquatic DNA** → you transform into the **Blacktip Reef Shark** and can now dive into the deep river currents leading to the ocean.

---

### Level 3 — The Bleached Coral Reef
**Form:** 🦈 Blacktip Reef Shark *(Pating)*

**What the world looks like:**
A fully underwater environment. Full 360-degree movement. The reef is pale and dead from ocean acidification.

**What you do:**
- Move fast and agile through the underwater world
- Defend a rare medicinal coral from waves of aggressive scavenger fish for 60 seconds *(Tower Defense-style)* to heal a dying sea turtle NPC
- Complete a stealth-combat minigame: navigate a narrow trench guarded by toxic Lionfish while dodging translucent plastic bags that look like jellyfish *(teaches visual mimicry and plastic pollution dangers)*

**Skill you earn:**
> **Lateral Line Sense** — A biological radar that draws visible lines on the screen showing exactly where invisible enemy attacks are coming from, based on water displacement.
> *(Based on the real lateral line organ that sharks and fish use to detect movement through water.)*

**Boss: The Lionfish-Starfish Chimera** *(bullet-hell style)*
- Fires waves of venomous spine projectiles
- Has a regenerative state — you must strike fast and dash away before it heals
- Requires precise movement to survive

**What unlocks next:**
Defeating the boss gives you **Heavy Mammal DNA** → you transform into the **Tamaraw** and can now smash through the reinforced boulders blocking the mountain path.

---

### Level 4 — The Highland Mountains *(Final Level)*
**Form:** 🐃 Tamaraw *(Mindoro Dwarf Buffalo)*

**What the world looks like:**
A cold, freezing highland summit polluted with e-waste. Heavy, dangerous, and unforgiving.

**What you do:**
- Move slowly but hit with incredible force — this form is about weight and endurance
- Find a mountain goat NPC pinned under boulders and surrounded by the most dangerous enemies in the game
- Charge through fortified enemy camps using your V-shaped horns to rescue the goat
- Complete a precision minigame: fight off enemies while charging a kinetic meter — stop a fast slider in a narrow green zone at the right moment to land a maximum-force strike that shatters the boulders

**Skill you earn:**
> **Horned Bulwark Charge** — A high-momentum charge attack with built-in damage resistance.
> *(Based on how the Tamaraw uses its mass and thick horns for both defense and offense.)*

**Boss: The Apex Chimera** *(True Final Boss)*

This is where everything comes together. The Apex Chimera constantly **mutates between the abilities of all four previous bosses** — toxic sludge from the Snail-Toad, charging from the Hog-Rodent, spine projectiles from the Lionfish-Starfish.

To win, you must **hot-swap between all four animal forms mid-fight** and counter each ability with the right form. Every form's skill matters. Every lesson you learned matters.

**Victory:**
Dr. Sterling patches the GenoMorph to 100% capacity. The Nature Volunteer becomes the true Keystone Species. The food web stabilizes. The water clears, the forest regrows, and the entire world lights back up with color.

## 💡 The Core Design Idea

The game is built around one principle:

> **You learn biology → you get stronger.**

Every skill in the game is based on a real animal adaptation. Every minigame teaches a real ecological concept. Every boss tests whether you actually understood what you learned.

The story reinforces this too — the ecosystem is broken because of human damage (pollution, logging, invasive species). The player restores it not by being a hero with magical powers, but by **understanding how nature actually works**.
